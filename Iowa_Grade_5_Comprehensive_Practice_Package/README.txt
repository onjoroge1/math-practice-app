Scope
This package contains 361 original questions across Vocabulary, Reading, Written Expression, Mathematics, Computation, Science, Social Studies, Spelling, Capitalization, and Punctuation. It follows the publicly described Iowa Assessments Level 11 complete-battery section counts and suggested times.

Important
This is independent practice material, not an official test. Practice raw scores cannot be converted to official national percentiles or grade-equivalent scores.
